Frequently Asked Questions

What is HandleKit?

HandleKit provides free, scientifically validated sensory screening labs—such as the clinical Pelli-Robson Contrast Sensitivity Test, Hearing Age biological threshold test, and Tinnitus sound therapy matcher—alongside professional creator and developer utilities. Everything runs in the browser without sign-ups.

Are the sensory and screening tools clinically accurate?

Yes. Our Contrast Sensitivity Test implements the official Pelli-Robson Log CS psychophysical benchmark, while our Hearing Age screener uses calibrated high-frequency tone synthesis (8 kHz to 20 kHz) to evaluate presbycusis. While not a substitute for an in-person medical diagnosis, they offer reliable, high-precision preliminary screenings.

Is my data private?

Privacy is fundamental to HandleKit. All sensory calculations, audio synthesis, password generation, and conversions execute 100% locally in your browser using Web Audio, Canvas, and WebAssembly APIs. Your personal inputs and test scores never leave your device.

Are all tools permanently free?

Yes, completely and permanently free. There are no premium tiers, subscriptions, paywalls, or trial periods. You will never be asked for an email address or credit card.

Can I suggest a new tool or screening assessment?

Absolutely. Send your ideas to contacthandlekit@gmail.com or use the contact page. New tools are developed based on community needs.
