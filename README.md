# HandleKit - Free Online Tools for Developers & Creators

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-handlekit.com-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://handlekit.com)

**[HandleKit](https://handlekit.com)** is a curated collection of free, browser-based tools designed to solve real problems for developers, writers, and digital creators. 

Almost all tools run entirely client-side (no data is sent to our servers), and they are completely free to use with no sign-ups or paywalls required.

## 🛠️ Featured Tools & Calculators

Here is an index of our most popular free tools. Feel free to use them, or even embed them on your own websites!

### 📱 [Social Media Safe Zone Visualizer](https://handlekit.com/social-media-safe-zones)
Preview exactly where TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts will place their UI buttons so you never cover your text again. Includes free downloadable transparent PNG templates.

### 📝 [Words to Pages Calculator](https://handlekit.com/words-to-pages)
Instantly convert word counts to pages based on custom fonts, font sizes, spacing, and margins. Perfect for authors, students, and copywriters needing exact page counts.

### 📐 [CSS Clamp() Calculator](https://handlekit.com/css-clamp-calculator)
Generate responsive, fluid typography rules instantly. Let our engine calculate the complex slope math for fluid clamp() functions so your fonts scale perfectly on every device.

### 🎨 [JND Color Perception Test](https://handlekit.com/jnd-test)
A scientific diagnostic tool based on Weber's Law to test your Just Noticeable Difference (JND) threshold for color vision.

### 👁️ [Contrast Sensitivity Test](https://handlekit.com/contrast-sensitivity-test)
Measure your functional vision threshold with this clinical-style Pelli-Robson chart simulator. 

### 🔐 [100 Worst Passwords of 2026](https://handlekit.com/100-worst-passwords)
A live database of the top 100 most hacked and dangerous passwords of the year, including how fast automated bots can crack them.

### 🔍 [Username Rules by Platform](https://handlekit.com/username-rules)
The ultimate cheat sheet for social media username requirements, including character limits and allowed symbols for every major platform.
